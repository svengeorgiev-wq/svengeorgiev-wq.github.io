ROI after ad spend · 30 days

ROI = (all royalties − ad spend) ÷ ad spend, as PublisherChamp calculates it, so organic sales count too. Ads-only ROI counts just the orders Amazon attributes to ad clicks, at each book's average royalty per unit. The truth lies between the two; for a decision on one campaign or target, look at the Ads-only figure and the last 7 days.
