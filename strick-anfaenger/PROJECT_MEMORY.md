# Mein Schal: Begleit-App zu „Stricken für Anfänger“ (30 Abende)

- App type: static, account-free PWA companion to „Stricken für Anfänger: Dein erster Schal in 30 Abenden“ (Munich Publishing, 138 pages, 7×10 in, successor of the S/W Advent edition B0HLY4G1XG). No backend, no analytics, no e-mail capture. State in `localStorage` under `strick-anfaenger-v1` (pos, done, ends, rid, bow, started, tab).
- URL: `https://tools.munichpublishing.de/strick-anfaenger/`. The QR code on book page 136 points here directly. Book page 136 says: scan, tap „Mit Abend 1 anfangen“.
- The old Advent app `/strick-schal/` stays unchanged for buyers of the Advent book. Both apps share the origin; each service worker only deletes its own caches (`strick-anfaenger-` vs `strick-schal-`).
- Data: `data.js` (window.SCHAL with `abende`, `help`) is generated by `export_app_30.py` in the book folder (Weihnachtsbuch Konzept/Neusatz). It takes the 324 rows from `../strick-schal/data.js` and the new texts from the book build (neu_abende.py, neu_schluss.py, neu_loesungen.py, manuell.py). Never edit by hand; the script asserts rows 1..324, stitch counts per evening and no „Türchen/Advent/Dezember“.
- 30 evenings: typ `anschlag` (Abend 1, 4 photos `assets/fotos/a01-*.webp` + link to the drawings view `#/anschlagen`, Maschen-Check 28), `kniff` (2–27), `wdh` (11, 13, 16, 17, 25: Wiederholen und Prüfen, no new kniff), `ende` (28–30). Split sections: 10/11 Perlmuster, 12/13 Hebemaschen, 15/16 Lochmuster, 17/18 Glatt mit Rettungsübung (half row 15 at Abend 18), 24/25 Schachbrett. Expected count: 27 after Abend 14 and 18, else 28.
- Book pages: opener with riddle 4k+5, „Am Schal“ with chart 4k+8.
- Views: Start, `#/reihe`, `#/check/N`, `#/abend/N/(kniff|schal|raetsel)`, `#/abende`, `#/hilfe`, `#/anschlagen`, `#/stand`, `#/installieren`, `#/info`.
- Shell changes: bump `?v=` in index.html and the cache name in sw.js.
